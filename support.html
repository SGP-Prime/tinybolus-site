TinyBolus Support

Help, questions, and how to reach us.

Contact

For any question, bug report, or feedback about TinyBolus, email tinybolus@aethersomnus.com. We aim to respond within a few working days.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your device and OS version, the app version (shown on the app's App Information screen), and the steps that led to the issue. Please do not include any patient-identifying information.

About the app

TinyBolus is a paediatric dosing reference for anaesthesia and emergency medicine, intended for use by qualified healthcare professionals. It presents weight- and age-scaled drug doses, equipment sizes, and physiological reference ranges as a starting point that must be verified against local protocols before use. It is a reference tool — it does not diagnose, prescribe, or make treatment decisions.

Common questions

How is a patient entered?

Enter the patient's age, and optionally a measured weight. When no weight is given, it is estimated from age using standard APLS age–weight formulas. A measured weight is always preferred when available.

Where do the doses come from?

Every clinical figure is drawn from published references and professional guidelines, and each is cited in the app's References section. The complete machine-readable formulary — doses, ranges, caps, and age brackets — is published for peer review and audit at github.com/SGP-Prime/tinybolusformulas.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All calculations run locally on your device with no network connection required. When online, the app quietly checks for an updated formulary so dose corrections can reach every device without a new app release; this update is one-way and sends no user or patient data.

Does the app collect my data?

No. TinyBolus collects, stores, and transmits no patient or user data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change what's shown?

The settings screen (top-right on the main screen) lets you hide or show individual drugs and whole sections, so the app can be tailored to your practice. A short orientation card is available from the help icon on first use.
